Tech & AI

Long-form pickAnthropic · Aug 14, 2026

Anthropic raises its own misalignment-risk rating — and shelves a model it won't name a release date for

Anthropic's second company-wide Risk Report bumps its estimate of catastrophic harm from misalignment in high-stakes settings from "very low" to "low," and discloses an unreleased internal model, Model 2 — somewhat more capable than frontier Mythos 5, with no plans to ship it externally. The report also says its AI R&D benchmark (CoBench v2) is nearing saturation, with Claude now authoring most of the code merged into its own production repos. Read straight, it's a primary source grading its own homework; read against the safety-cases literature (today's paper pick), it's also a preview of what "trust us" governance looks like before anyone outside the lab can independently check the claims.

Paper of the day

Safety Cases for Frontier AI

Argues frontier labs need a rigorous, shared methodology for "safety cases" — structured arguments for why a system is safe enough to deploy — and that none yet exists. Reads like a direct commentary on what's missing from today's Anthropic report.

Why these

Anthropic's report is about as close to a primary "here's how we think about our own catastrophic risk" document as this beat gets, which is exactly why it's paired with a paper on why nobody outside the lab can yet independently check that kind of claim. The round-up otherwise tracks where governance is actually being tested in public right now — Meta's glasses, AI crawlers already ignoring the access rules regulators are still drafting — against the sheer scale of capital (DeepSeek's pricing, Nvidia's stake, the $220B infrastructure debt) now riding on all of it working.

Long-form pickMeduza · Aug 14–15, 2026

Russia rejects Ukraine's Black Sea truce proposal — and says the West broke its word last time

Ukraine offered Russia, via a third party, a mutual halt to attacks on civilian shipping and ports in the Black Sea, as strikes on tankers and grain terminals threaten global food supply. Russia's Foreign Ministry says it never received a formal proposal through official channels — and, more substantively, that it sees no grounds for a "half-measure" that gives Kyiv a reprieve without the sanctions relief Moscow says it was promised after the 2022–2023 grain-corridor deal. No vessel has entered Odesa-region ports since late July.

Washington shifts Cuba strategy from military threat to economic strangulation

The Trump administration has quietly deprioritized military options against Havana in favor of sanctions — over 240 imposed since January, roughly 80-90% of energy imports cut via tanker interceptions — betting economic pressure forces reform faster than force would.

Bloomberg

Indonesia earthquake death toll climbs past 50 as rescue widens

A magnitude-7.7 quake off Flores island triggered landslides that cut the 700km Trans-Flores Highway; roughly 5,000 people are displaced and over 341 aftershocks have followed. Rescue teams are still clearing blocked roads to reach isolated villages.

Bloomberg · NPR · France 24

West Bank settler-violence standoff drags into another day

A days-long confrontation continues without Israeli intervention, adding to a pattern critics say shows official reluctance to rein in settler violence against Palestinians even as international attention stays fixed on Gaza.
